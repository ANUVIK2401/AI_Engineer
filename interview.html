How to use the bank

Turn practice into actual recall

The goal is not to read all 487 answers. It is to quickly notice which ideas you can explain and which ones still collapse under pressure.

01

Warm up fast

  • Pick one topic set.
  • Answer 5 questions without opening the answer first.
  • Mark only what you genuinely reviewed.
02

Listen for weak spots

  • Notice where you get vague.
  • Bookmark the questions that expose it.
  • Return to the concept page if the mechanism is missing.
03

Practice the stronger version

  • Read the takeaway first.
  • Use the point-by-point answer to rebuild your explanation.
  • Come back later and try again from memory.

Best way to use this bank

  • When you miss a question, fix the concept before you memorize the wording.
  • When an answer feels long, learn the takeaway line first and the detail second.
  • When a topic keeps failing, bookmark 3-5 questions and revisit them daily.

What a strong answer usually includes

  • The mechanism: what actually happens.
  • The tradeoff: what you gain and what it costs.
  • The failure mode: what breaks when the assumption stops holding.
